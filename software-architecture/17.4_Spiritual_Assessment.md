# Purpose

The Spiritual Compatibility Assessment evaluates how faith is lived and integrated into everyday life.

Its objective is not to measure a person's spiritual worth or relationship with God. Instead, it identifies areas that may influence compatibility within a Christian marriage.

---

# Dimensions Measured

The MVP evaluates four dimensions:

1. Importance of Faith
2. Spiritual Practices
3. Vision of Marriage
4. Church & Community Engagement

These dimensions provide a practical understanding of how two people may build a shared spiritual life.

---

# Assessment Structure

- Total questions: **10**
- Estimated completion time: **2–3 minutes**
- Response scale: **5-point Likert**

1. Strongly disagree
2. Disagree
3. Neutral
4. Agree
5. Strongly agree

---

# Question Bank

## Importance of Faith

1. My faith influences my daily decisions.
2. I want God to be at the center of my future marriage.
3. Spiritual compatibility is essential when choosing a spouse.

## Spiritual Practices

4. Prayer is a regular part of my life.
5. Reading the Bible is an important habit for me.
6. I value praying together as a couple.

## Vision of Marriage

7. I believe marriage is a lifelong covenant.
8. I desire to resolve conflicts according to biblical principles.

## Church & Community Engagement

9. I am actively involved in a local church.
10. I believe serving others is an important part of the Christian life.

---

# Scoring

Each answer receives a value from 1 to 5.

For every dimension:

- Calculate the raw score.
- Normalize the result on a 0–100 scale.

The overall Spiritual Compatibility Score is the average of all normalized dimensions.

---

# Matching Usage

The spiritual assessment contributes to the global compatibility score by comparing the similarity of each member's spiritual priorities and expectations.

The objective is to identify couples likely to share compatible spiritual lifestyles and values.

---

# Ethical Principles

The platform must never:

- classify someone as a "better Christian";
- judge spiritual maturity;
- replace pastoral guidance;
- discourage relationships solely because of assessment scores.

Assessment results are informational and should always support, not replace, personal discernment.
