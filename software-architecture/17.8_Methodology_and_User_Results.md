# 1. Eden™ Profile Philosophy

The MVP avoids assigning rigid personality labels.

Instead, each member receives a personalized profile composed of:

- Strongest qualities
- Areas for growth
- Personality tendencies
- Spiritual tendencies
- Relationship tendencies

The objective is to promote self-awareness rather than categorization.

---

# 2. Profile Structure

Each profile is built from three assessment scores:

## Personality

Measures communication style, adaptability, emotional stability and reliability.

## Spiritual Compatibility

Measures the place of faith, spiritual practices, vision of marriage and church engagement.

## Relationship Compatibility

Measures communication, conflict management, emotional availability and commitment.

Together, these create a unique Eden™ profile.

---

# 3. Matching Integration

The matching engine follows these steps:

1. Verify mandatory filters (age, gender, location, marital preferences, etc.).
2. Compare the three normalized assessment scores.
3. Calculate an overall compatibility percentage.
4. Rank compatible members.
5. Present the best matches to the user.

No assessment is used on its own to accept or reject a match.

---

# 4. Compatibility Result

The user sees a simple compatibility indicator.

| Compatibility | Meaning |
|--------------:|---------|
| 90–100 | Excellent |
| 75–89 | Strong |
| 60–74 | Good |
| 45–59 | Moderate |
| Below 45 | Limited |

This indicator is informational and does not replace personal discernment.

---

# 5. User Report

After completing the assessments, each member receives a report containing:

- Overall compatibility profile
- Personality summary
- Spiritual summary
- Relationship summary
- Main strengths
- Areas to develop
- Brief explanation of how matching works

The report must be encouraging, balanced and free of judgment.

---

# 6. Privacy

Individual answers are never shared.

Other members only see:

- Compatibility percentage
- General compatibility indicators

Raw scores and detailed responses remain private.

---

# 7. Design Principles

The Eden™ Method must always be:

- Simple
- Transparent
- Respectful
- Explainable
- Extendable

The MVP deliberately favors clarity over algorithmic complexity.

---

# Future Evolution

Future versions may introduce:

- richer profile archetypes;
- AI-assisted explanations;
- personalized recommendations;
- dynamic compatibility weighting;
- premium relationship reports.

These enhancements are outside the scope of the MVP.
