# Purpose

This assessment evaluates core personality tendencies that influence communication, decision-making and daily interactions in a relationship.

It is not intended to diagnose psychological conditions or assign permanent labels.

---

# Dimensions Measured

The MVP evaluates four dimensions:

1. Communication Style
2. Emotional Stability
3. Openness & Adaptability
4. Responsibility & Reliability

Each dimension is assessed using three questions.

Total: **12 questions**.

---

# Response Scale

All questions use a 5-point Likert scale:

1. Strongly disagree
2. Disagree
3. Neutral
4. Agree
5. Strongly agree

---

# Question Bank

## Communication Style

1. I express my thoughts openly.
2. I actively listen before responding.
3. I remain respectful during disagreements.

## Emotional Stability

4. I stay calm under pressure.
5. I manage my emotions without hurting others.
6. I recover quickly after disappointment.

## Openness & Adaptability

7. I adapt easily to change.
8. I appreciate viewpoints different from mine.
9. I enjoy learning new ways of doing things.

## Responsibility & Reliability

10. People can rely on me to keep my commitments.
11. I plan ahead before making important decisions.
12. I usually finish what I start.

---

# Scoring

Each question is scored from 1 to 5.

For each dimension:

- Calculate the raw score.
- Normalize the result on a 0–100 scale.

An overall personality score is then calculated as the average of the four normalized dimensions.

---

# Personality Summary

Instead of assigning rigid personality types, the MVP provides a simple summary:

- Primary strengths
- Development opportunities
- Overall personality score

This avoids oversimplification while providing useful feedback.

---

# Matching Usage

The personality assessment contributes to the compatibility calculation by comparing the four dimension scores between two members.

The goal is to identify complementary and compatible interaction styles rather than identical personalities.
