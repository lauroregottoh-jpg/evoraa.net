# Purpose

The Relationship Compatibility Assessment measures relational habits, expectations and interpersonal behaviors that are likely to influence the quality and stability of a future marriage.

It does not predict relationship success. Instead, it highlights areas of compatibility and potential differences that deserve discussion.

---

# Dimensions Measured

The MVP evaluates four relationship dimensions:

1. Communication
2. Conflict Management
3. Emotional Availability
4. Commitment & Partnership

Each dimension contributes equally to the overall relationship compatibility score.

---

# Assessment Structure

- Total questions: **12**
- Estimated completion time: **3–4 minutes**
- Response scale: **5-point Likert**

Scale:

1. Strongly disagree
2. Disagree
3. Neutral
4. Agree
5. Strongly agree

---

# Question Bank

## Communication

1. I express my needs respectfully.
2. I listen carefully before responding.
3. I am comfortable discussing difficult topics.

## Conflict Management

4. I seek solutions rather than winning arguments.
5. I apologize when I realize I am wrong.
6. I remain respectful even during disagreements.

## Emotional Availability

7. I easily express care and affection.
8. I am comfortable talking about my emotions.
9. I encourage open emotional communication.

## Commitment & Partnership

10. I believe important decisions should be made together.
11. I value mutual support in everyday life.
12. I am willing to make personal sacrifices for the success of a relationship.

---

# Scoring

Each answer receives a value between 1 and 5.

For each dimension:

- Calculate the raw score.
- Normalize the score on a 0–100 scale.

The overall Relationship Compatibility Score is the average of the four normalized dimensions.

---

# Matching Usage

This assessment compares the relationship profiles of two members.

The objective is not to identify identical personalities, but to estimate whether their relational habits and expectations are likely to work well together.

The Relationship Compatibility Score is combined with:

- Personality Assessment
- Spiritual Compatibility Assessment

to produce the overall compatibility score displayed by the platform.

---

# User Feedback

At the end of the assessment, users receive:

- their strongest relationship strengths;
- areas that may require intentional growth;
- a brief explanation of how these results contribute to compatibility.

The feedback should encourage reflection rather than judgment.
